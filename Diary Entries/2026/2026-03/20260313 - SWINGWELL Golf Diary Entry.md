DURING
• What showed up unexpectedly: Thin contact on chips and pitches was a recurring problem that needed to be brought under control mid-session.
• One adjustment made: Held the head down through the entire swing, past the point of ball contact, which corrected the thin strikes noticeably.
• One moment of trust (or loss of it): Trusting the head-down cue paid off – ball-striking improved meaningfully once the key was identified and committed to.

POST
• What actually mattered today: Diagnosing and fixing thin contact, and finishing the lag putting session on a strong upward trend.
• Evidence (not judgment): 2 thin out of 42 chips; 4 thin out of 45 pitches; 14/24 made at 12 feet; lag putting improved from 9/16 to 26/32 at 30 feet.
• One takeaway for next session: Lead with the head-down cue from the very first swing – don’t wait for thin contact to appear before applying it.
