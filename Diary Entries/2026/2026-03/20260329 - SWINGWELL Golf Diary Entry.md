# SWINGWELL - Golf Diary Entry - 2026-03-29

SWINGWELL – Golf Diary Entry 20260329

Location / Context: PGA Tour Superstore (indoor facility / simulator bay) 
Round / Practice: Practice session

PRE
• Intent (1 sentence): Work through a structured club progression from wedges to hybrid, using the slapshot drill as the foundation.
• Mental state (GREEN / YELLOW / RED): GREEN
• One commitment: Execute the slapshot drill first before hitting full shots with each club.

DURING
• What showed up unexpectedly: The 4 hybrid was noticeably less consistent, likely because the pre-shot drill routine was skipped before picking it up.
• One adjustment made: Focused on stopping hand rotation through and after contact to prevent hands turning over too much.
• One moment of trust (or loss of it): Trust was found with the gap wedge and 9 iron — the starts to the right with a draw back were working; trust was lost slightly with the 4 hybrid when the routine was abandoned.

POST
• What actually mattered today: Keeping hands in the correct position through the swing, and recognising the direct link between skipping the drill and losing consistency.
• Evidence (not judgment): Spin rate dropped from high 300s to low 300s; gap wedge and 9 iron shapes were repeatable; 4 hybrid was markedly less consistent without the drill.
• One takeaway for next session: Run the slapshot drill before every club, including the hybrid — no exceptions.
