# SWINGWELL - Golf Diary Entry - 2026-03-07

SWINGWELL – Golf Diary Entry 20260307

Date
Location / Context: PGA Tour Superstore – Indoor Practice Range
Round / Practice: Practice Session (~40 minutes)

PRE
• Intent (1 sentence): Work through the bag systematically, starting with wedges and building up to driver.
• Mental state (GREEN / YELLOW / RED): YELLOW
• One commitment: Progress club by club with deliberate attention to distance control and contact.

DURING
• What showed up unexpectedly: The driver started poorly — arms-dominant swinging created inconsistent contact early in that block.
• One adjustment made: Shifted from arm-swinging to rotating toward the target, then further refined by bringing the hands in closer.
• One moment of trust (or loss of it): Trust was found, not lost — committing to the rotation and hands adjustment led to noticeably better contact and straight ball flight.

POST
• What actually mattered today: The ability to self-diagnose and self-correct with the driver mid-session.
• Evidence (not judgment): Wedges were tight (1–2 misses outside 5 yards per 20 balls); hybrid showed good trajectory despite wider dispersion; driver finished with good contact and straight flight after two distinct adjustments.
• One takeaway for next session: Begin the driver block with the rotation-toward-target cue from the first swing rather than waiting for contact to deteriorate.
