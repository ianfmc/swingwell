# SWINGWELL - Golf Diary Entry - 2026-03-10

SWINGWELL – Golf Diary Entry 20260310

Location / Context: Golf Center, Palm Desert — Evening session
Round / Practice: Practice (bucket of balls + short game)

PRE
• Intent (1 sentence): Work on hand movement — dropping and bringing hands from the inside to produce a consistent draw shape.
• Mental state (GREEN / YELLOW / RED): YELLOW
• One commitment: Stay committed to the inside hand path and trust the swing shape.

DURING
• What showed up unexpectedly: A tendency to look downrange before ball contact — occurring roughly 30% of the time and disrupting strike quality.
• One adjustment made: In the bunker, shifted focus to getting deeper under the ball and taking more sand, correcting an early pattern of thin contact.
• One moment of trust (or loss of it): Loss of trust — early bunker shots came out thin, suggesting hesitation before the adjusted technique took hold.

POST
• What actually mattered today: The discovery that bringing the hands from the inside out on pitch shots produced a noticeably consistent and repeatable shot shape.
• Evidence (not judgment): ~15 bunker shots converted onto the green once the adjusted technique was applied; the majority of full swings (³70%) were solid despite the head-lifting pattern.
• One takeaway for next session: Reinforce the inside-out hand path across all short game shots — it is already producing results and is worth building on deliberately.
