## DURING – Awareness

* **What showed up unexpectedly:**  
The strongest shots occurred when I felt as though I was swinging significantly out to the right. What felt exaggerated produced a neutral-to-slightly in-to-out club path.

* **One adjustment made (technical or mental):**  
Focused on taking the club back straighter and more neutrally while maintaining an in-to-out delivery.

* **One moment of trust (or loss of it):**  
After several consecutive solid strikes, I stopped trying to manipulate the swing and trusted the "right-field" feel.

---

## POST – Reflection (No Judgement)

* **What actually mattered today:**  
Discovering a repeatable feel that produced the desired club path and contact quality.

* **Evidence (observable, countable, factual):**
  * Approximately 30 balls hit
  * One mishit
  * Average distance approximately 100 yards
  * Club path generally between 3° out-to-in and 3° in-to-out
  * Majority of shots finished within approximately 20 feet of the center line
  * Slight left-tail pattern observed

* **One takeaway for next session:**  
Continue using the "swing out to the right" feel and observe whether the resulting path remains neutral without introducing additional swing thoughts.
