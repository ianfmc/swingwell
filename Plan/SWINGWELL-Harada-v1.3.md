Every pillar now has an explicit focus level. This describes where attention goes, not skill, results or the desired level of proficiency. The handicap objective, eight pillars, 64 exact commitments and their v1.2 priorities are unchanged.

Active drives the current practice priorities and review. Development receives planned work supporting those priorities. Maintenance retains established skills or habits. Future means deliberately deferred; no pillar currently has that status. The existing 49 unassigned commitments remain unassigned, not Future. Commitment counts stay 4 active, 6 development and 5 maintenance, plus those 49 unassigned.

Pillar focus is explicitly assigned, not aggregated from commitment priorities. An Active pillar can contain Development, Maintenance or unassigned commitments. Including a commitment in a session does not automatically change its strategic priority. Recorded evidence and the linked plan provide feedback in its detail pane.

The overview uses one plain focus label per pillar and a restrained colored bottom edge. A dark outline marks selection; keyboard focus has its own visible ring. The full matrix and pillar details use the same focus data. Definitions are available through a compact disclosure. No counts or chips are added to the overview.
