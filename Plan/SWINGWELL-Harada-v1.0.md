# SWINGWELL — Canonical Harada Matrix v1.0

**Status:** Approved in conversation, 2026-11-22.
**Goal:** Handicap index **13.0 by June 30, 2027**.
**Baseline:** 19.9. **Latest GHIN-verified:** 17.9 on November 1, 2026.

> SWINGWELL must not consume time that could otherwise be spent playing or practicing golf.
> The purpose of SWINGWELL is to become a better golfer without sacrificing the joy of playing golf.
> Raise the floor, preserve the ceiling.

The 9×9 Harada layout uses the goal at the center of the central 3×3 block; eight pillar names in its surrounding cells. Each surrounding 3×3 block places its pillar at the center, with the eight commitments in the surrounding cells. IDs are stable references; the UI can map their visual positions without changing IDs.

## Ball Striking (BS)

**Outcome:** Predictable low point and centered contact across the bag.

| ID | Approved commitment |
|---|---|
| BS-01 | Consistently control the low point ahead of the ball. |
| BS-02 | Produce centered clubface contact across the irons. |
| BS-03 | Maintain effective pressure transfer through impact. |
| BS-04 | Establish a repeatable setup and ball position for each club. |
| BS-05 | Maintain consistent contact quality across successive shots. |
| BS-06 | Transfer reliable contact from partial swings to full swings. |
| BS-07 | Preserve contact quality when changing clubs and targets. |
| BS-08 | Reduce costly thin, fat and other contact errors during rounds. |

**Performance guidance:** Initial practice benchmark: at least 8 of 10 acceptable contacts in a randomized iron set; define acceptable contact before testing.

**Outcome:** Predictable approach shots that increase GIR, especially from 150–180 yards.

| ID | Approved commitment |
|---|---|
| AP-01 | Establish reliable carry-distance windows for short and mid irons. |
| AP-02 | Establish reliable carry and dispersion windows for long-approach clubs, including hybrids and fairway woods. |
| AP-03 | Control distance dispersion to reduce approaches finishing significantly short or long. |
| AP-04 | Control lateral dispersion to keep intended greens within a realistic shot pattern. |
| AP-05 | Maintain accurate, evidence-based carry distances for all approach clubs. |
| AP-06 | Select clubs and targets using actual carry distances and expected dispersion. |
| AP-07 | Maintain approach accuracy when switching clubs, distances and targets. |
| AP-08 | Improve on-course greens in regulation, particularly from 150–180 yards. |

**Performance guidance:** Initial GIR benchmark: 3 per 9 holes. Long-approach calibration: 7 of 10 within agreed carry and lateral windows; define windows by distance/club. Track 5i, 6i, 7i, 8i, 4H, 3W and future fairway woods as supporting evidence, not separate commitments.

| ID | Approved commitment |
|---|---|
| SG-01 | Produce consistent contact and predictable rollout on basic chips. |
| SG-02 | Control carry distance and trajectory on pitch shots. |
| SG-03 | Develop reliable greenside bunker escapes, with predictable distance and enough control to create putting opportunities. |
| SG-04 | Select appropriate short-game shots based on lie, landing area and available green. |
| SG-05 | Control landing spots and rollout across different clubs and trajectories. |
| SG-06 | Maintain effective short-game execution from rough, tight lies, uneven ground and other challenging conditions outside bunkers. |
| SG-07 | Transfer short-game skills from controlled practice to randomized, on-course situations. |

**Performance guidance:** Initial development benchmarks: scrambling at least 30%; 7 of 10 basic chips inside 6 feet from a defined lie; 9 of 10 bunker escapes safely onto green; at most one costly short-game mishit per nine holes. Bunker and non-bunker difficult lies are distinct.

| PU-02 | Develop consistent pace control across putting distances. |

| PU-04 | Improve conversion consistency from five to ten feet. |

| ID | Approved commitment |

| CM-03 | Make club selections using reliable carry distances rather than maximum distances. |
| CM-04 | Evaluate risk and reward before attempting aggressive or low-percentage shots. |

| CM-06 | Adapt shot selection to wind, elevation, lie and course conditions. |

**Performance guidance:** Initial goals: zero penalty strokes per nine; at most one costly execution error per nine across all clubs; zero compounded errors. Baseline decision quality separately from execution. Decision evaluation uses information available before shot. Mental commitment mechanics belong to P7.

## Practice Discipline (PD)

**Outcome:** Practice deliberately, measure honestly, and improve without making golf another job.

| ID | Approved commitment |
|---|---|
| PD-01 | Maintain a realistic four-session weekly Flight Plan, adapting to available time and conditions. |
| PD-02 | Begin each practice session with a clear purpose and process-based intention. |
| PD-03 | Use measurable success criteria to distinguish improvement from repetition. |
| PD-04 | Balance technical development, randomized practice and on-course transfer. |
| PD-05 | Record every completed practice session and round in the SWINGWELL diary, with detail proportional to the evidence and learning value. |
| PD-06 | Complete the Saturday Review to identify progress, inconsistencies and emerging priorities. |
| PD-07 | Complete the Sunday Plan to translate findings into the next four-session Flight Plan. |
| PD-08 | Complete the Monthly Matrix Update to keep commitments and priorities aligned with the handicap objective. |

**Performance guidance:** Four sessions are a planning baseline, not a quota. Record every completed practice and round, in brief when appropriate; no backlog or guilt. Saturday learn, Sunday prepare, monthly align.

## Operating ceremonies

- **Saturday Review — Learn:** Review diaries, rounds and evidence; persist a weekly review and justified commitment-evidence changes; recommend priorities.
- **Sunday Plan — Prepare:** Use Saturday findings to publish four Apple Notes-ready sessions in `weekly-plan.json`.
- **Monthly Matrix Update — Align:** Reassess goal, pillars, priorities and objectives; version the matrix only when evidence warrants strategic change.

## Evidence and maintenance rules

- Separate measured facts, hypotheses, and proposed benchmarks.
- Do not treat missing baseline as poor performance.
- Use dated evidence and link back to diary or round source when available.
- Keep drills in the Flight Plan, not in strategic commitments.
- A four-session plan is a flexible baseline, never a quota.
- Record every completed round and practice session, with depth proportional to learning value.
- Do not expose GHIN membership numbers or sensitive information in public GitHub Pages.
