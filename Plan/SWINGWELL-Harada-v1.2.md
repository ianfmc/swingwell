## Approved two-minute arrival routine

Use before practice or a round, within the existing warm-up:

- **30 seconds:** Feel your feet; notice tension and your GREEN/YELLOW/RED state without judging it.
- **60 seconds:** Breathe naturally. When expectations show up, acknowledge them and return attention to breathing.
- **30 seconds:** Set the intention: **“Some shots will miss. Choose, commit, then give the next shot my attention.”**

Success means taking the pause and choosing the intention. It does not require feeling calm or hitting the first ball perfectly. No separate tracking system or additional practice session is introduced.
