Archived guidance · June 2026

Practice priorities recorded in June 2026

01

Make 33% GIR repeatable

Keep the 100–150 yard approach block. Record reps, carry window, and shots inside 20 feet.

02

Protect the easy-swing cue

Use it with PW and driver; measure the dispersion gain against the distance cost instead of chasing speed.

03

Train the back nine

Add a fatigued nine-hole simulator finish or late-session pressure set. Watch holes 10–18 separately.

04

Remove one 3-putt

Maintain lag putting with a pressure finish. Turning 34 putts into 33 is the cleanest immediate stroke.
